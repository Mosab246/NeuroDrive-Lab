NeuroDrive Lab

Race against drivers that taught themselves to drive: PPO (reinforcement learning) and NEAT (evolved networks). They only see 9 distance sensors, their speed and their steering, like you see the road.

Opponents
Drive with ↑ ↓ ← → or WASD. R restart, Esc menu. Leaving the track ends your race.
Ghost race: cars pass through each other. Physics and AI run in your browser, ported 1:1 from the Python simulator.
